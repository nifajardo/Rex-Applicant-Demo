## Before You Start

### What You'll Need
✓ A valid email address  
✓ A password (at least 8 characters)  
✓ The following documents (scanned or digital copies):
  - Endorsement Letter from your school/organization
  - Application Letter (your motivation letter)
  - Report Card or academic records
  - Proof of parental income
  - Enrollment Form from your school
  - Copy of your School ID
  - Copy of your Birth Certificate
  - Copy of your Voter's Registration Record

### System Requirements
- A computer, tablet, or smartphone with internet access
- Any modern web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge)
- Stable internet connection

### Important Notes
- The application process takes approximately **30-45 minutes**
- You can save your profile and come back later to complete documents
- All your information is secure and confidential
- Deadline information will be displayed on the application home page

## Creating Your Account

### Option 1: Sign Up with Email

**Step 1:** Go to the application website and click **"Sign Up"**

**Step 2:** You'll see the registration form with these fields:
- **Email Address** - Use an email you check regularly
- **Full Name** - Enter your complete name as it appears in your ID
- **Password** - Create a strong password (mix of letters, numbers, and symbols)
- **Confirm Password** - Re-enter your password to verify

**Step 3:** Click the checkbox to **accept the terms and conditions**

**Step 4:** Click **"Register"** button

**Step 5:** A confirmation email will be sent to your email address
- Check your inbox (or spam folder)
- Click the verification link in the email
- Your account is now active!

### Option 2: Sign Up with Google (Faster!)

**Step 1:** On the registration page, click **"Sign Up with Google"**

**Step 2:** Select which Google account to use

**Step 3:** Google will ask for permission to share your information
- Click **"Allow"**

**Step 4:** You're automatically logged in!
- Your full name and email come from your Google account

## Completing the Learning Module

### What is This?

Before applying for the scholarship, you'll need to complete an **Image Presentation** and a **Quiz**. This helps us ensure all applicants understand the scholarship program and the application process.

### Completing the Image Presentation

**Step 1:** After logging in, you'll see an **Image Presentation** screen

**Step 2:** This is an informational presentation about the scholarship program
- Watch the presentation (approximately 5 minutes)
- Look at all the images and information provided
- This will help you understand the scholarship better

**Step 3:** Click **"Next"** or **"Continue"** to move through the slides

**Step 4:** Once finished, click **"Proceed to Quiz"** or similar button

### Completing the Quiz

**Step 1:** The quiz will appear with **multiple-choice questions**

**Step 2:** For each question:
- Read the question carefully
- Select your answer
- Move to the next question

**Step 3:** The quiz typically has **5-10 questions** covering:
- Scholarship eligibility
- Application requirements
- Important dates and processes

**Step 4:** Once you complete all questions, click **"Submit Quiz"**

**Step 5:** Your score will be displayed
- **Pass:** You can proceed to the dashboard (most applicants pass!)
- **Fail:** You can retake the quiz

✓ After passing, you'll have access to your **Dashboard** to start the application!

## Step 1: Complete Your Profile

This is where you enter your personal and academic information.

### How to Start

**Option A - From Home:**
1. Click **"Complete Your Profile"** button on the home page
2. Go to the **"Profile"** section

**Option B - From Menu:**
1. Click **"Profile"** in the sidebar menu
2. Click **"Edit Profile"** button

### Profile Information to Enter

#### Personal Information
- **Full Name** - Your complete legal name
- **Email Address** - Your active email
- **Phone Number** - Mobile or home phone
- **Date of Birth** - Your birthdate (MM/DD/YYYY)
- **Home Address** - Complete street address
- **Barangay** - Your home barangay

#### Academic Information
- **School/University** - Name of your school
  - *If your school isn't listed, select "Other" and type the name*
- **Degree Program** - Your current program (e.g., Bachelor of Science)
- **Academic Year** - Your current year (1st Year, 2nd Year, etc.)
- **GWA (Grade Weighted Average)** - Your GPA in percentage format
  - *Example: 87.5 (NOT 3.5)*
- **Scholarship Type** - Select which scholarship you're applying for:
  - EXCEPTIONAL (GWA 85%+)
  - ACADEMIC (GWA 85%+)
  - ATHLETIC (national-level athletes)
  - ARTISTIC (special arts programs)
  - SUMMER CLASS (6-9 units)
  - TESDA (technical education)
  - EDUCATIONAL ASSISTANCE (GWA 82%-84%)

#### Family Information
- **Father's Name** - Your father's full name
- **Mother's Name** - Your mother's full name

### Uploading Your Profile Picture (Optional)

1. Click on the **profile picture area**
2. Select an image from your computer
3. Crop/adjust if needed
4. Click **"Upload"**

### Saving Your Profile

1. After entering all information, click **"Save Profile"** button
2. You'll see a **"Profile saved successfully"** message
3. Your information is now saved!

## Step 2: Upload Required Documents

Now you'll upload the documents needed for your application.

### How to Access

1. Click **"Requirements"** in the sidebar menu
2. You'll see a list of all required documents

### Required Documents

Here are the **8 documents** you need to upload:

#### 1. **Endorsement Letter**
- **What it is:** A letter from your school, barangay, or organization supporting your application
- **File format:** PDF, JPG, PNG, DOC, DOCX
- **Tips:** Make sure it's signed and dated

#### 2. **Application Letter**
- **What it is:** Your personal motivation letter explaining why you need the scholarship
- **File format:** PDF, DOC, DOCX (recommended)
- **Tips:** Keep it professional, 300-500 words

#### 3. **Report Card/Grades**
- **What it is:** Your latest school report card or transcript
- **File format:** PDF, JPG, PNG
- **Tips:** Should show your recent grades and GWA

#### 4. **Proof of Parental Income**
- **What it is:** Document showing your family's income (tax return, payslip, barangay certificate)
- **File format:** PDF, JPG, PNG
- **Tips:** Any official document showing income is acceptable

#### 5. **Enrollment Form**
- **What it is:** Proof that you're enrolled in your current school
- **File format:** PDF, JPG, PNG
- **Tips:** School letterhead enrollment certificate works great

#### 6. **School ID Photocopy**
- **What it is:** A copy of your school identification card
- **File format:** JPG, PNG (photo of ID)
- **Tips:** Make sure both front and back are visible if applicable

#### 7. **Birth Certificate Photocopy**
- **What it is:** Certified copy of your birth certificate
- **File format:** PDF, JPG, PNG
- **Tips:** Should be clear and readable

#### 8. **Voter's Registration Record**
- **What it is:** Proof that you're registered to vote in Batangas City
- **File format:** PDF, JPG, PNG
- **Tips:** Shows residency in Batangas City

### How to Upload Each Document

**Step 1:** Locate the document name in the Requirements list

**Step 2:** Click the **"Upload"** or **"Add File"** button

**Step 3:** One of two options:
- **Drag and drop:** Drag your file onto the upload area
- **Click to browse:** Click and select file from your computer

**Step 4:** Select your file from your computer

**Step 5:** The file will upload automatically
- You'll see a **progress bar** showing upload progress
- Once complete, you'll see a **checkmark** ✓

**Step 6:** You can:
- **Preview** the file by clicking the eye icon
- **Download** it by clicking the download icon
- **Delete** it and re-upload if needed by clicking the trash icon

### Tips for Successful Uploads

✓ **File Size:** Keep each file under 10MB (smaller is better)  
✓ **Quality:** Make sure documents are clear and readable  
✓ **Format:** JPG, PNG, and PDF work best  
✓ **Order:** Upload all pages in the correct order  
✓ **Naming:** The system will name files automatically

## Troubleshooting & FAQs

### I forgot my password. What do I do?

1. Go to the login page
2. Click **"Forgot Password?"**
3. Enter your email
4. Check your email for the reset link
5. Create a new password and log in

### My file failed to upload. What's wrong?

**Common causes:**
- File is too large (max 10MB)
- Internet connection dropped
- File format not supported

**Solution:**
1. Try again
2. Use a smaller file size
3. Use JPG, PNG, or PDF format
4. Check your internet connection

### I can't see my uploaded documents.

1. Refresh the page (F5)
2. Wait a few seconds and check again
3. Try uploading again
4. Clear your browser cache

### My school isn't on the list. What do I do?

1. Scroll to the bottom of the school list
2. Select **"Other"**
3. Type your school's name manually

### How do I change my profile information after submitting?

Once you submit your application, most information is locked. However:
1. You can still update some information
2. Contact the REX Education office for major changes
3. Submit an updated feedback form with your changes

### How long will my application take to process?

- **Initial review:** 2-4 weeks
- **Final decision:** 4-8 weeks
- **Notification:** Via email and phone

You'll receive updates as your application progresses.

### Where is the REX Education office?

**Location:** REX Education Support Center  
**Phone:** Contact information will be provided after submission  
**Email:** You'll receive contact details in your confirmation email

### What if I didn't pass the quiz?

1. Don't worry - you can retake it!
2. Review the learning materials
3. Click **"Retake Quiz"**
4. Answer the questions again
5. Most applicants pass on retake

### I'm not a resident of Batangas City. Can I still apply?

The scholarship is primarily for **Batangas City residents**. 

One of the required documents is a **Voter's Registration Record** from Batangas City, which proves residency. 

If you don't have this, contact the REX Education office to discuss your eligibility.

### Can I apply for multiple scholarships?

You can select one primary scholarship type for your application. 

If you're eligible for multiple types, you can discuss this with the REX Education staff after submission.

### The chatbot isn't answering my question.

1. Try rephrasing your question
2. Check the FAQ section in the chatbot
3. The chatbot will direct you to contact staff for complex questions
4. Use the contact information in your confirmation email

### I submitted my application but didn't get a confirmation email.

1. Check your spam/junk folder
2. Wait 5-10 minutes for the email to arrive
3. Check the application dashboard for status
4. Contact the REX Education office if you still don't see it

## What Happens Next?

### Timeline

| Step | Timeframe | What to Expect |
|------|-----------|-----------------|
| **Application Received** | Immediately | Confirmation email |
| **Initial Review** | 2-4 weeks | Staff reviews your application |
| **Interview/Evaluation** | 4-6 weeks | Possible interview or verification call |
| **Final Decision** | 6-8 weeks | Email notification of results |
| **Onboarding** | 8-12 weeks | Scholarship agreement & requirements |

### How You'll Know the Status

1. **Email Updates** - Regular status updates via email
2. **Dashboard** - Check the "Application Status" section
3. **Phone Call** - Important updates communicated by phone
4. **Official Notification** - Final decision sent via email

### Possible Outcomes

✓ **Approved** - Congratulations! Follow instructions for onboarding  
⏳ **Under Review** - Your application is being reviewed  
❌ **Denied** - You'll receive explanation; you can reapply next cycle  
❓ **Need More Information** - Contact the REX Education office to clarify  

### If You're Approved

You'll receive:
1. **Scholarship Award Letter**
2. **Scholarship Agreement to sign**
3. **Next steps and requirements**
4. **Contact information for support**

### If You Have Questions During Processing

**Contact the REX Education Office:**
- **Location:** REX Education Support Center
- **Email:** (provided in confirmation)
- **Phone:** (provided in confirmation)
- **Reference Number:** Use this when contacting them (from your confirmation)
